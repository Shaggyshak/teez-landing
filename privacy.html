Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 August 2026 Last updated: 3 August 2026 Controller: Teez, Inc.

1. Scope — what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Teez, Inc. ("Teez", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and discloses information in connection with the Teez AI Underwriting service. It applies to the service as a whole, specifically:

  • Teez AI Underwriting for Microsoft Excel — our Office add-in, distributed through Microsoft Marketplace (AppSource) and installed into Microsoft Excel on Windows, Mac and the web.
  • The Teez web application at app.teez.live, which shares the same account and data.
  • Our website at teez.live.

Teez AI Underwriting is a commercial real estate underwriting tool. It reads deal documents you provide — offering memoranda, trailing-twelve financials, rent rolls and similar — extracts the figures from them, and writes those figures into your Excel model. Understanding that flow is the key to understanding this policy: the documents and spreadsheet content you put into Teez are the core of what we process.

2. Information we collect

CategoryWhat it isWhy we have it
Account information Your email address, password (stored only as a salted hash — we never see or store it in readable form), and any name you supply. To create your account, authenticate you in the add-in and the web app, and contact you about the service.
Deal documents Files you upload — offering memoranda, T12s, rent rolls, leases and similar — together with the text and figures extracted from them. This is the product's function: to read these documents and underwrite from them.
Spreadsheet content Cell values, ranges, formulas and sheet structure from the workbook you have open, sent from the add-in when you ask the agent to act. So the agent can understand your model's layout and write values into the right cells without breaking your formulas.

Website analytics Aggregate visit data collected via Google Analytics on teez.live. To understand which pages are useful. This runs on our marketing website only — not inside the Excel add-in.

We do not collect payment card details. If and when you pay for Teez, card data is handled by our payment processor and never reaches our servers.

3. What the add-in reads from your workbook

The add-in requests the ReadWriteDocument permission from Microsoft Office. That permission is what allows Teez to read your spreadsheet and write results back into it.

In practice this means:

  • Workbook content is read when you ask the agent to do something — it is not continuously monitored or streamed in the background.
  • What is transmitted is the cell content and structure needed for the request you made.
  • Teez writes into your workbook only the values it has told you it will write. Your formulas are preserved.
  • The add-in does not read files on your computer other than the workbook you have open, and has no access to your other Office documents, email or calendar.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide the service — parse your documents, run the underwriting agent, and populate your model.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account and workspace secure.
  • Maintain the audit trail that lets you trace every figure back to its source.
  • Diagnose faults, investigate abuse, and improve reliability and extraction accuracy.
  • Communicate with you about the service, including support responses and material changes.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your deal documents or model content with other customers, and we do not use them for advertising.

5. AI processing

Teez uses large language models to read documents and generate underwriting output. To do this, the contents of your documents and relevant spreadsheet context are transmitted to our AI provider, Anthropic, through its commercial API.

Under the terms on which we access that API, your content is processed solely to return a result to us, and is not used to train the provider's models. We do not train any model of our own on your deal documents or workbook content.

AI output can be wrong. Teez is an underwriting aid, not a substitute for your own review — please see section 6 of the End User License Agreement.

7. Storage, location and security

Data is stored on infrastructure located in the United States. If you access Teez from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed there.

We protect information with measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, access controls that isolate each customer's data to their own account, hashed credential storage, and restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify affected users without undue delay if a breach affecting personal information occurs.

8. Retention

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Deal documents, extracted data and session records are kept until you delete them or close your account, so that your workspace and audit trail remain available to you.
  • Technical and diagnostic logs are kept for a limited period for security and debugging, then discarded.

When you close your account, we delete or irreversibly anonymise your personal information and deal content within a reasonable period, except where we must retain records to meet a legal obligation. To request deletion, email support@teez.live.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Residents of California, the EEA and the UK have specific statutory rights along these lines.

You can exercise any of these by emailing support@teez.live. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them. You may also delete individual deals and their documents yourself from within the product at any time.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not handled your information properly, you have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

10. Confidentiality of third-party information

Deal documents frequently contain information about people who are not Teez users — tenants named on a rent roll, for instance. Where we process such information, we do so as a processor acting on your instructions, and you remain responsible for having the right to provide it to us. We do not use that information for any purpose other than providing the service to you.

11. Children

Teez is a professional tool intended for business use. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are material, we will give you notice by email or through the product before they take effect. Continued use of Teez after a change takes effect means you accept the revised policy.
