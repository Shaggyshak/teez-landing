Pricing questions.

What to know before you pick a tier.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The Excel add-in starts free so you can run it on a real file before paying. After the trial it bills as a subscription per seat.

What is the difference between the add-in and the operating system?

The add-in is the underwriting agent inside Excel, on its own. The operating system connects that same engine to your inbox and your phone, so deals can be sent in by email or text and results come back the same way, without a dashboard. It is priced to your stack rather than per seat.

How is Deep Underwriting priced?

Per deal, not per seat. We collect the real numbers from the broker, property manager, lender, insurance and tax sources, re-underwrite against your return hurdle and deliver a before-and-after model or a full IC memo.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel the add-in subscription any time and you keep every model it filled. To change your plan or close your account, email support@teez.live.
