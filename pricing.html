Pricing questions.

What if I run more than 10 deals a month?

Contact us and we'll price a plan to your deal volume. Everything in the standard plan is included.

Who gets the $75 price?

First-time users. The standard price is $100 a month for 10 deals.

How is Deep Underwriting priced?

Per deal, separately from the subscription. We collect the real numbers from the broker, property manager, lender, insurance and tax sources, re-underwrite against your return hurdle and deliver a before-and-after model or a full IC memo.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time and you keep every model Teez built. To change your plan or close your account, email support@teez.live.
