Team questions.

How is this different from an AI assistant in Excel?

A general AI assistant can do the math once you hand it the inputs. Teez finds and checks the inputs: it looks the building up in our property records, pulls sale and rent comps, flags rent regulation and tax abatements, and cites every number it writes.

Will it work with our template?

Yes. The add-in scans the open workbook, maps the inputs it extracted to the right cells, and writes only to input cells, so a model it has never seen is safe to fill.

How are numbers sourced?

Every value written into the sheet carries a citation to the page or cell it came from. The activity view in the task pane shows the trail for any figure.

What does it cost?

It bills as a subscription per seat, with no platform fee and no minimum. Current details are on the pricing page.

Who sees my deal documents?

You and the people you invite. Documents and workbook content are processed to return a result to you and are never shared with other customers or used to train models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
