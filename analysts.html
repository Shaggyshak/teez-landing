Team questions.

Do we have to replace our deal management or data room tools?

No. Teez isn't a pipeline, CRM or data room, and it doesn't ask you to move your deals into a new system. It works on the underwriting itself: it fills and updates the Excel model your team already uses, with the research and sources behind each input. Keep the tools you use to track deals and diligence.

Can Teez use our own deal history and capex data?

Yes. That is the point. Teez connects your past deals and operating results to the assumptions in your next acquisition: what renovations cost per unit, the premium your renovated leases actually achieved, and the repairs, turnover, concessions and collections at the properties you own. The data is reconciled to consistent categories and shown next to the assumption it supports, for example your actual repairs spend at comparable properties beside the broker's line, with exclusions explained. You accept or override it, and the approved case is kept so you can compare what you underwrote with what happened.

Is this capex or asset management software?

No. We don't run budgets, invoices, vendors or construction schedules. We use cost and performance history to answer the underwriting question: what to assume, and what to pay, on the next acquisition.

Will it work with our template?

Yes. The add-in scans the open workbook, maps the inputs it extracted to the right cells, and writes only to input cells, so your formulas stay yours.

How are numbers sourced?

Every value written into the sheet carries a citation to the page or cell it came from, and the activity view in the task pane shows the trail for any figure. The research behind the inputs comes from our property records and public data: we look the building up, pull sale and rent comps, flag rent regulation and tax abatements where we have them, and check the broker's numbers against all of it. See what's in the record.

What does it cost?

Your first three deals are free. After that the subscription covers 10 deals a month, and teams that run more are priced to their deal volume. Details are on the pricing page.

Who sees my deal documents?

You and the people you invite. Documents and workbook content are processed to return a result to you and are never shared with other customers or used to train models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
