Analyst questions.

What modeling shops ask before they try the add-in on a client file.

How does the Teez Excel add-in install?

Teez runs inside Excel on Windows, Mac and the web. Once installed it appears as Teez Underwriting on the Home tab of the ribbon; select it to open the task pane and sign in. If your organisation manages add-ins centrally, your IT administrator may need to approve it. See support for details.

Will it work with a client’s template I have never opened before?

Yes. The add-in scans the open workbook, builds a dependency graph of its formulas, and maps the inputs it extracted to the right cells. It writes only to input cells, so an unfamiliar model is safe to fill.

Which asset types and document formats are supported?

Multifamily, industrial and retail models. Documents can be PDF, Excel or CSV: offering memorandums, rent rolls in any layout, T-12s and lender term sheets.

How are numbers sourced?

Every value written into the sheet carries a citation to the page or cell it came from. The activity view in the task pane shows the trail for any figure, so when a client asks where a number came from, you can show them.

What does the add-in cost?

It starts free, then bills as a subscription per seat. There is no platform fee and no minimum. Cancel and you are back to exactly where you started. Current details are on the pricing page.

Does Teez see or store my clients’ data?

Documents and workbook content are processed to return a result and kept only as needed to provide the audit trail. They are never shared with other customers or used to train models. The Privacy Policy covers exactly what the add-in reads from a workbook.
