Broker questions.

What investment-sales teams ask before they send us a listing.

What is a BOV, and what does Teez produce?

A broker opinion of value (BOV) is the valuation package an investment-sales broker prepares to win a listing: in-place and pro forma NOI, a cap-rate-based value range, comps and a recommended ask. Teez fills your firm’s BOV template from the rent roll and T-12 and returns the value range by text, with the model ready in Excel.

Do I have to change my firm’s BOV or OM template?

No. Teez works inside the Excel template your back office already pitches from, including branded layouts. It writes the inputs and leaves your formulas, formatting and branding untouched.

Can I really run it from my phone?

Yes. Text Teez the rent roll and T-12 as attachments and it replies with the cap rate, value range and a link to the filled model. You never have to open the file until you want to.

Where do the comps come from?

Teez pulls public market context and works alongside the comp set your team maintains. It does not replace your market read; it removes the hours between receiving the rent roll and having a defensible number.

How many pitches can one team run?

As many as the inbox delivers. Teams that build fifty to two hundred BOVs a year by hand typically win a fraction of them. Teez makes each pitch cheap enough that the volume stops being the constraint.

How is it priced for brokerage teams?

Brokers run Teez through the operating system tier, priced to your stack rather than per seat. Bring a listing and we will build the pitch math in your template while you watch, then talk pricing.
