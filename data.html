The data behind Teez

Every deal starts with
the facts already in hand.

Teez keeps a growing record of public data on multifamily buildings. Send an address and it comes back with what's known about it, each fact with its source and date.

What's in
the record.

We collect it from public sources, match it to the building, and keep it current, so the model doesn't start from the offering memo.

Our records

Building facts

Units, year built, size, lot and ownership for multifamily buildings across our markets.

Our records

Recorded sales

What traded, when and for how much, so price per unit and exit cap are checked against real closings.

Our records

Rent regulation and tax abatements

Where available: which buildings are likely rent-regulated, which carry an abatement and when it expires.

Our records

Violations and landmark status

Where available: open and historical code violations and designations, flagged as capex and renovation risk.

Public data

Public loan data

Where available: loan balance, maturity and distress signals from the public data of the commonly known agencies.

Our records

Physical building

Where available: footprint and number of stories, so a renovation scope isn't priced from the offering memo alone. With a renovation planned and no budget, Teez adds a labeled exterior ballpark you can edit.

Your data

Your deal history

Every deal you underwrite with Teez sharpens the next one. It stays yours and is never shared with anyone else.

Where it covers.

Major metro areas and a growing list of Sunbelt markets. Coverage map coming soon.

Where a market doesn't publish a field, Teez says so instead of guessing.

Every number shows
its source.

Each figure in a Teez underwrite carries where it came from and when it was last updated. If the data behind a number is partial or approximate, the model says so. You never have to wonder whether a number is a record or a guess.
