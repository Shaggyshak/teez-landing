Sponsor questions.

What do I need to send?

The offering memorandum, the rent roll and the T-12 are enough for a first pass. Add a lender term sheet and Teez fills the debt assumptions too. Text them or forward the broker's email.

How long does it take?

Minutes, not days. Re-running a deal when the rate or rent forecast moves takes about the same, which matters because assumptions go stale fast.

Do I need Excel or any new software?

No. The numbers and summary arrive by text or email, and a web dashboard holds the detail. The Excel model is attached whenever you want to open it.

Can Teez use my own model?

Yes. Send your Excel template once and Teez fills it on every deal, writing to input cells only. If you don't have one, you get ours.

Who sees my deal documents?

You and the people you invite. Documents and workbook content are processed to return a result to you and are never shared with other customers or used to train models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
